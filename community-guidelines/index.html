8HI APK Community Guidelines

8HI APK is designed for verified platform information and respectful adult discussion. Impersonation, unsafe links and guaranteed-result claims do not belong here.

01

Respectful participation

02

No unofficial files

No false guarantees

Do not promote bots, prediction tools or certain-result claims.

Adults only
